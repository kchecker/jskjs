jsKjs vs Alpine.js

Pulse brings Alpine's "state in the markup" model to jsKjs: kj-data, kj-text, kj-show, kj-model, @event, :attr, components and stores. It is deliberately smaller than Alpine.

Choose jsKjs when…
  • Your components are toggles, forms, wizards, counters, tabs and carts.
  • You want reactivity plus a jQuery API, hypermedia and animation in one file.
Choose Alpine.js when…
  • You need x-for keyed list rendering or x-if templates.
  • You rely on magics ($watch, $dispatch, $refs, $nextTick) or plugins (persist, focus, intersect, collapse).
  • You want x-transition or a CSP-safe build.

Same component, renamed

HTML
<!-- Alpine -->
<div x-data="{ open: false }"><button @click="open = !open">Menu</button><ul x-show="open">…</ul></div>
<!-- jsKjs -->
<div kj-data="{ open: false }"><button @click="open = !open">Menu</button><ul kj-show="open">…</ul></div>
