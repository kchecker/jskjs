jsKjs vs GSAP

Motion gives you the GSAP-style API for everyday UI animation — tweens, transforms, easing, stagger, timelines — in a fraction of the size. GSAP remains the tool for animation-heavy work, and it is now free.

Choose jsKjs when…
  • Your motion is UI feedback: entrances, hovers, toasts, modals, staggered lists, small sequences.
  • You want it in the same 8.1 KB file as your DOM, requests and state.
Choose GSAP when…
  • You need ScrollTrigger, Flip, Draggable, SplitText, MorphSVG or any other plugin (GSAP and all its plugins are free since Webflow's 2025 change).
  • You need playback control: pause, reverse, seek, progress, timeScale, repeat and yoyo.
  • You animate colours, SVG attributes, 3D transforms or hundreds of concurrent tweens.
